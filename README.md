# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, and regional trends from an e-commerce dataset. It provides a centralized view of key business metrics through dynamic visualizations and slicers, enabling users to explore data across different time periods, regions, product categories, and shipping modes.

Built with a modern dark-themed layout, the dashboard helps stakeholders quickly identify sales trends, top-performing categories, regional performance, and customer insights for better decision-making.

## Key Features

* **Interactive Filters:** Analyze data by Year, Month, Region, Country, State, Product Category, Customer Segment, Ship Mode, and Order Priority.
* **KPI Cards:** View Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Average Discount at a glance.
* **Monthly Sales Trend:** Track sales performance over time with a dynamic line chart.
* **Product Category Analysis:** Compare sales and profit across product categories.
* **Shipping Mode Distribution:** Understand order distribution by different shipping methods.
* **Regional Performance:** Identify top-performing regions through bar charts and a geographic sales map.
* **Customer Segment Analysis:** Compare sales and profit across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
* **Key Insights Panel:** Highlights important business findings such as top-performing regions, categories, and shipping modes.

## Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop**
* **Power Query** for data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** for calculated measures and KPIs
* Interactive visualizations including Cards, Line Charts, Column Charts, Donut Charts, Maps, Tables, and Slicers

## Business Insights

The dashboard enables users to:

* Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify high-performing product categories and customer segments.
* Analyze regional sales contributions.
* Evaluate shipping mode preferences.
* Explore monthly sales patterns using interactive filters.
* Make data-driven decisions through real-time dashboard interactions.
